## 📖 About The Project
**DriveFleet** is a modern, responsive full-stack web application built to streamline the car rental process. Whether you are looking to rent a vehicle for a weekend getaway or list your own car to earn extra income, DriveFleet provides a secure, intuitive, and efficient marketplace.

### ✨ Key Features
- **Smart Fleet Discovery:** Search and filter vehicles by model and car type.
- **Secure Authentication:** JWT-based login/registration with HTTPOnly cookie protection for private route security.
- **Owner Management:** Full CRUD capabilities for car owners to add, update, and delete their listings.
- **Booking System:** Track your rentals and manage your trip history effortlessly.
- **Dynamic UI:** Responsive design that looks great on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
- **Real-time Interaction:** Integrated booking counters and interactive modals.
